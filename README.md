# Tableau Visual Analytics — Laptop Sales & Sports Equipment

Two Tableau case studies analysing retail sales data using visual analytics techniques.

---

## 📊 Project 1 — Laptop Sales Analysis

**Business Context:**
A leading retail chain in London with 15+ stores wanted to define product strategy 
and pricing policies to maximise projected revenues.

**What was built:**
- Dynamic Top-N parameter to display top laptop configurations by sales
- Average retail price trend analysis over time
- Store-level pricing consistency analysis with laptop filters
- Seasonal sales patterns by month and day
- Configuration grouping using Combined Fields (864 → 32 groups across 5 parameters)
- Custom geographic distance calculated field to analyse average customer travel per store
- Store-view Dashboard with interactive store filter
- Tableau Story presenting end-to-end analysis

## 📊 Project 2 — Sports Equipment Sales Analysis

**Business Context:**
A global sports equipment retailer wanted to analyse combined revenue across 
countries and product types for strategic decision-making.

**What was built:**
- Market share analysis by retailer country (% of total revenue)
- Revenue vs Quantity correlation analysis using CORR() calculated field
- Quarterly revenue trends by country across 3 years
- Dual axis chart comparing Revenue and Quantity by Product Type
- Sets to isolate Top 5 Product Types by quantity sold
- Total Revenue and Profit by country using calculated field (Profit = Revenue × Gross Margin)
- Dynamic Top-N parameter for top products by year
- Pareto chart using running sum table calculations identifying product types driving 80% of quantity
- Year-on-year percentage growth by product category
- 3-month moving average compared against actual monthly revenue
- Parameter-driven view dynamically switching between Revenue, Profit, and Quantity
